# Chef Uma's Digital Kitchen

Build a polished, premium one-page restaurant website demo for a small local Thai food business called:

CHEF UMA’S KITCHEN

This is a DEMO for a real local business, so the website should feel authentic, warm, personal, and commercially realistic — NOT like a generic AI-generated restaurant template.

IMPORTANT:
Do not invent services, locations, prices, ordering systems, reviews, or business information.
Use the exact business information and menu provided below.

The website is primarily designed to turn visitors into phone/text orders and make the business look significantly more professional than its current Facebook-only presence.
